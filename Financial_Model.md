## Financial Model (Year 1)

This document provides a projected financial model for the first year of operation of the autonomous meta-SaaS platform. This model is intended to be used by the `CFO Agent` to manage the company's finances.

---

### Projected Revenue

Revenue is projected to come from three sources:

1.  **Meta-Platform Subscriptions:** Revenue from users who subscribe to the meta-SaaS platform to build their own SaaS products.
2.  **Revenue Share:** A percentage of the revenue generated by the SaaS products that are spawned and managed by the platform.
3.  **SaaS Flips:** Revenue from selling successful SaaS products on marketplaces like Acquire.com.

| Quarter | Meta-Platform Subscriptions | Revenue Share | SaaS Flips | Total Revenue |
|---------|-----------------------------|---------------|------------|---------------|
| Q1      | $1,500                      | $500          | $0         | $2,000        |
| Q2      | $5,000                      | $2,500        | $10,000    | $17,500       |
| Q3      | $15,000                     | $7,500        | $25,000    | $47,500       |
| Q4      | $30,000                     | $15,000       | $50,000    | $95,000       |
| **Total** | **$51,500**                 | **$25,500**   | **$85,000**| **$162,000**  |

### Projected Costs

Costs are projected to come from three main sources:

1.  **Infrastructure:** Hosting costs for the meta-SaaS platform and all spawned SaaS products.
2.  **LLM API Usage:** Costs associated with using large language models from providers like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google.
3.  **Third-Party Services:** Costs for other services such as email providers, analytics tools, and other APIs.

| Quarter | Infrastructure | LLM API Usage | Third-Party Services | Total Costs |
|---------|----------------|---------------|----------------------|-------------|
| Q1      | $3,000         | $5,000        | $1,000               | $9,000      |
| Q2      | $6,000         | $10,000       | $2,000               | $18,000     |

| **Total** | **$45,000**    | **$75,000**   | **$15,000**          | **$135,000**|
