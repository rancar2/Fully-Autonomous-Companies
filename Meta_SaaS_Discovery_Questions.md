## 2. Meta-SaaS Discovery Questions

This document contains the key questions the AI assistant asked to align on the scope and architecture of the meta-SaaS platform before proceeding with a detailed roadmap and architecture.

---

### 🧭 1. Target User Clarity

*   Who is the intended _user_ or _beneficiary_ of the meta-SaaS?
    *   A) Aspiring founders with no-code experience?
    *   B) Developers who want to spin up SaaS apps faster?
    *   C) Passive investors or holding companies?
    *   D) AI agents (as users) building for other AI agents?

---

### 🏗 2. Output Definition

*   What exactly does the platform _deliver_ or _manage_ for the SaaS businesses it creates?
    *   Full production deployment (code + cloud infra + CI/CD)?
    *   MVP landing page + waitlist + analytics + pricing logic?
    *   App backend + marketing automation + Stripe integration?
    *   App + customer support + iteration loop?

---

### 🧠 3. Scope of Autonomy

*   What degree of _autonomous operation_ are you targeting per spawned SaaS?
    *   Just ideation + prototype generation?
    *   Fully running PaaS with zero human involvement?
    *   Agent-managed user feedback, billing, and scaling?

---

### 🧩 4. Level of Customization

*   Do users define the SaaS they want (niche, feature list)?
*   Or does the platform autonomously:
    *   Identify market gaps
    *   Build micro-SaaS tools
    *   Launch, monetize, and iterate?

---

### ⚙️ 5. Underlying Platform Goals

*   Is this platform itself intended to:
    *   Be a product-led SaaS (charge users to build)?
    *   Be a profit-maximizing agent (spawn & grow its own SaaS ventures)?
    *   Be open-source infrastructure for AI-native startups?
    *   Sell spawned SaaS assets (M&A model or "SaaS flipping")?

---

### 🔁 6. Business Loop Design

*   What’s the monetization loop?
    *   Subscription for usage?
    *   Revenue share from spawned SaaS?
    *   Licensing agents/templates?
    *   Internal portfolio of revenue-generating apps?

---

### 🧱 7. Infrastructure Preferences

*   Any tech stack or platform preferences?
    *   Hosting (Hetzner, Vercel, Fly.io)?
    *   DB (SQLite, Supabase, etc.)?
    *   Agent framework (CrewAI, LangGraph, custom)?
    *   Code generation language? (Ruby, JS, Python, Elixir?)

---

### 🛡️ 8. Legal Risk Tolerance

*   Are you planning to incorporate in a jurisdiction favorable to:
    *   Autonomous agents?
    *   DAOs or AI-run businesses?
    *   Reduced liability on agent-driven actions?

---

### 📊 9. Metrics for Success

*   What would success look like in 90 days?
    *   10 SaaS MVPs launched?
    *   First paying customer of a spawned SaaS?
    *   Fully autonomous feedback/improvement cycle in a SaaS?

---

### 🔒 10. Security & Ownership

*   How is ownership handled?
    *   Does the user own the output SaaS code?
    *   Does the platform keep IP and license access?
    *   Can users export / fork / self-host their SaaS?
